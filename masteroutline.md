## What the Framework Is

WLM starts with one question: *if you had to derive reality from the absolute minimum number of assumptions, what would those assumptions force?*

The answer I arrived at is a three-layer architecture. There's a layer of invisible generative structure — I call it D. There's a layer that renders that structure into experience — I call it E. And there's the only layer we ever actually observe: the difference between D and E. That visible layer is S, defined formally as Δ(D,E).

From that starting point, the framework tries to derive everything else. Physical constants. Dimensional structure. Consciousness. The logic of history. Why religious traditions converge. Why measurement yields a point instead of a wave.

It's an ambitious program. Some of it is rigorous; some of it is still evolving. All of it is published openly for anyone to examine, criticize, or build on.
